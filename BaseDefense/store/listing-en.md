# Google Play listing (English)

## Title (up to 30 characters)

Base Defense: Tower Defense

## Short description (up to 80 characters)

24 levels, 10 towers with upgrade paths, bosses, weather and daily challenges.

## Full description (up to 4000 characters)

Enemies march along the road to your base. Build towers, upgrade them and let nobody through!

Base Defense is a classic tower defense game you can play with one hand: short levels, clear rules and plenty of tactics. It works offline and has no ads.

★ 24 LEVELS IN THREE WORLDS
The Green Valley, a desert swept by sandstorms and the frozen north with blizzards and icy roads where enemies slide faster. Every map has its own route, forks and bridges.

★ 10 TOWERS AND 20 SPECIALISATIONS
Turret, shotgun, rockets, flamer, SAM site, tesla coil, sniper, mines right on the road, radar and a quarry that earns coins. At level four every tower picks a path: minigun or piercer, cluster or heavy rocket, napalm or cryo and seven more pairs.

★ 12 ENEMIES AND BOSSES
Runners, mutant swarms, armored brutes, medics, drones, shieldbearers, ghosts that only show up near a radar, sappers that jam your towers and brood mothers that burst into a swarm. At the end of a world: an APC and a helicopter.

★ ABILITIES
Call an airstrike anywhere on the map, freeze every enemy, or repair the base when things get hot.

★ THREE DIFFICULTIES AND CHALLENGES
Easy, normal and hard. Every level has a challenge: no selling, at most six towers, no leaks and more. Win it for a medal.

★ PLENTY AFTER THE CAMPAIGN
Endless mode with a high score, a daily challenge with new twists every day, 25 achievements, a codex of enemies and towers, and permanent upgrades bought with stars and medals.

★ COMFORTABLE
Pinch to zoom the map, undo a misplaced tower, play at ×2 and ×3 speed, and your battle is saved when you leave the game. English and Russian.

The graphics come from the free Kenney packs (kenney.nl, CC0), and the sounds and music are synthesized inside the game. No internet needed.
