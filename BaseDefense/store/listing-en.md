## Full description (up to 4000 characters)

Enemies march along the road to your base. Deploy soldier squads, upgrade them and let nobody through!

Base Defense is a tower defense game you can play with one hand, with squads of soldiers in sandbag pits for towers: short levels, clear rules and plenty of tactics. It works offline and has no ads.

★ 32 LEVELS IN FOUR WORLDS
The Green Valley, a desert swept by sandstorms, the frozen north with blizzards and icy roads where enemies slide faster, and a volcano with rivers of lava where eruptions rain lava bombs. Every map has its own route, forks and bridges.

★ 10 SQUADS AND 20 SPECIALISATIONS
Riflemen, shotgunners, a bazooka team, flamethrowers, a Stinger team, Tesla troopers, snipers, minelayers with mines right on the road, a radar post and a supply squad that earns coins. Every squad has its own uniform and weapons, and each level puts another soldier in the pit. At level four a squad picks a path: minigun or piercer, cluster or heavy rocket, napalm or cryo and seven more pairs.

★ 16 ENEMIES AND THREE BOSSES
Runners, mutant swarms, armored brutes, medics, drones, shieldbearers, ghosts that only show up near a radar, sappers that jam your squads and brood mothers that burst into a swarm. On the volcano: salamanders in fire suits, diggers that burrow underground and juggernauts nothing can stop. The bosses: an APC, a helicopter and a titan whose stomp knocks out your squads.

★ COMMANDERS WITH RANKS
Lead a hero across the map with a tap: he fires on his own, levels up to five during the battle and has his skills. Sergeant Volkov with a grenade and a rallying cry, engineer Kovaleva with a sentry gun and an EMP, captain Grom with an armor piercer and a grenade barrage. Battle after battle a commander rises to rank 10, grows stronger and at rank 5 learns a third skill. New commanders join for campaign stars.

★ CAMOUFLAGE
Desert and winter camouflage for winning their worlds: it repaints your squads' pits and dresses the commanders in new uniforms.

★ ABILITIES
Call an airstrike anywhere on the map, freeze every enemy, or repair the base when things get hot.

★ THREE DIFFICULTIES AND CHALLENGES
Easy, normal and hard. Every level has a challenge: no selling, at most six squads, no leaks and more. Win it for a medal.

★ PLENTY AFTER THE CAMPAIGN
A weekly operation of 15 heavy waves with bosses and medals for the lives you keep; a daily challenge with new twists every day; monthly seasons full of rewards; endless mode with a high score; 31 achievements, a codex of enemies and squads, and permanent upgrades bought with stars and medals. Reminders about the daily challenge if you want them.

★ COMFORTABLE
A training battle with step-by-step tips for your first time, pinch to zoom the map, undo a misplaced squad, play at ×2 and ×3 speed, and your battle is saved when you leave the game. English and Russian.

The graphics come from the free Kenney packs (kenney.nl, CC0), and the sounds and music are synthesized inside the game. No internet needed.
