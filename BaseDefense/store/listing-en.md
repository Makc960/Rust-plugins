40 levels in five worlds, 10 squads, 4 commanders, bosses and a story.

## Full description (up to 4000 characters)

Enemies march along the road to your base. Deploy soldier squads, upgrade them and let nobody through!

Base Defense is a tower defense game you can play with one hand, with squads of soldiers in sandbag pits for towers: short levels, clear rules and plenty of tactics. It works offline and has no ads.

★ 40 LEVELS IN FIVE WORLDS, AND A STORY
The Green Valley, a desert swept by sandstorms, the frozen north with icy roads, a volcano raining lava bombs, and the ruins of a city where enemies come down two roads at once and ambush you out of the cellars. Between the worlds, chapters of a story told in pictures: the war against the Horde.

★ 10 SQUADS AND 20 SPECIALISATIONS
Riflemen, shotgunners, a bazooka team, flamethrowers, a Stinger team, Tesla troopers, snipers, minelayers with mines right on the road, a radar post and a supply squad that earns coins. Each level puts another soldier in the pit, and at level four a squad picks a path: minigun or piercer, napalm or cryo and eight more pairs.

★ 19 ENEMIES AND FOUR BOSSES
Runners, mutant swarms, brutes, medics, drones, shieldbearers, ghosts, sappers, brood mothers, fireproof salamanders, diggers, juggernauts, bikers and flame tanks. The bosses: an APC, a helicopter, a titan whose stomp knocks out your squads and an airship that drops paratroopers.

★ FOUR COMMANDERS WITH RANKS
Lead a hero across the map with a tap: they fire on their own, level up during the battle and have their skills. Sergeant Volkov, engineer Kovaleva, captain Grom and lieutenant Sokolova, a sniper and a medic. Battle after battle a commander rises to rank 10 and at rank 5 learns a third skill. New commanders join for campaign stars.

★ CAMOUFLAGE
Desert and winter camouflage for winning their worlds: it repaints your squads' pits and dresses the commanders in new uniforms.

★ ABILITIES, DIFFICULTIES AND CHALLENGES
An airstrike, a freeze on every enemy and a repair for the base. Three difficulties, and a challenge on every level for a medal: no selling, no leaks and more.

★ PLENTY AFTER THE CAMPAIGN
A boss rush against the clock with medals; a weekly operation of 15 heavy waves; a daily challenge with new twists; three orders from headquarters every day and supplies for every day you play; monthly seasons full of rewards; endless mode; 34 achievements, a codex and permanent upgrades bought with stars and medals.

★ COMFORTABLE
A training battle with tips, pinch to zoom the map, undo a misplaced squad, faster play, and your battle is saved when you leave the game. English and Russian.

The graphics and many sounds come from the free Kenney packs (kenney.nl, CC0); the orchestral music is played with the GeneralUser GS sound bank. No internet needed.
